Five facts, scrolling

What 25 years of precinct data show

Scroll through the argument; the figure on the right follows along. Then open the full tools.

01 · Scale

Scattered returns, one file

1.04 million polling-station rows — every Russian federal election, 2000–2024 — cleaned and harmonized into a single longitudinal dataset the CEC never offered.

02 · Harmonization

The number changes; the identity doesn't

Precincts are renumbered between elections. RED links the fragments into one stable identity — the work that makes every other view below possible. See how →

03 · A changing system

Off-site voting grows into the system

Voting that is harder to observe — off-site and early — becomes a steadily larger share of the electorate over the period.

04 · Convergence

Regions stop looking different

The spread of incumbent support across regions narrows as the system consolidates — visible as a falling cross-regional standard deviation. Explore regions →

05 · Read with care

Anomaly indicators, not verdicts

The turnout × share fingerprint reveals bunching and tails. These are statistical indicators for analysis — never proof of fraud at any single precinct.

What RED lets you do

The primary source — the Central Election Commission's website — is not reachable from outside Russia and never offered bulk downloads. RED integrates, cleans, and harmonizes the scattered record into a single longitudinal dataset.

Coverage

Study every federal cycle since 2000

Six presidential and five parliamentary elections: electorate, turnout by voting mode, invalid/valid ballots, and votes for every party list and presidential candidate.
